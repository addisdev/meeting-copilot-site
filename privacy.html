Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

In short: the developer of Meeting Copilot collects nothing. Your recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your devices. Nothing leaves them unless you choose to send it somewhere, meeting by meeting.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Meeting Copilot has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party code, and it does not track you. The developer has no server that receives your data and cannot see anything you record.

What stays on your device

Recordings, transcripts, summaries, actions, the people in your meetings and your notes are stored inside the app on your device. On iPhone, finished meetings are protected by iOS Data Protection, so they can't be read while the iPhone is locked. The app does not sync them anywhere. Like other apps' data, they are included in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup, if you have backups turned on. Deleting the app deletes them from the device.

Permissions and why they are asked for

  • Microphone: to record a meeting, only after you tap Record. iOS shows that it is recording.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on your device. If your device can't transcribe a language on the device, Apple's speech recognition may process that audio under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Calendars: to fill in a meeting's title and attendees, and to add follow-up events you ask for.
  • Reminders: to add the actions you choose to a Meeting Copilot list.
  • Call audio, on the Mac: to record a Teams or Zoom call you start recording.

Summaries are written on your device with Apple Intelligence where it is available.

When something leaves your device

  • Your own server, if you set one up. Under Settings you can enter the address of a server you run. A meeting's transcript is sent to it only if you agree for that meeting, and its audio only with a separate yes. That server, and any AI service it uses, is run by you and under your control, not the developer's.
  • A folder you choose for Mac recordings. If you save recordings from the Mac to iCloud Drive or another storage service, they are stored there under that service's terms.
  • Things you share. Exports and recaps go wherever you send them.

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations differ by place, and some require everyone's consent. Tell the people in a meeting that you are recording it.

Children

Meeting Copilot collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, the policy and the app's App Store privacy details will say so first.
