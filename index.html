Meeting notes you can check

Meeting Copilot records your meetings, in the room or a Teams or Zoom call, and turns them into a summary, the decisions, the actions and who owns them, and the questions still open. Every point links to the line in the transcript it came from, so you can tap it and hear it.

Private by default. Recordings and transcripts stay on your iPhone, encrypted. There is no account, no analytics and no third-party code. Read the privacy policy.

Common questions

Does it need an account or a server? No. Transcripts and summaries are made on your iPhone. A summary uses Apple Intelligence where your iPhone has it, and is shorter where it doesn't.

What is the backend setting for? People who run their own server for longer meetings. Leave it empty otherwise. When it is set, a meeting's transcript goes to that server only if you agree for that meeting, and its audio needs a separate yes.

Will it keep recording if I lock the screen? Yes. It pauses for a phone call and saves audio in short pieces as it goes, so a crash or a flat battery loses seconds, not the meeting.

How do I get a call from my Mac onto my iPhone? The Mac companion records Teams and Zoom calls. Save them to a folder in iCloud Drive, then choose that folder in the iPhone app under Settings, Mac recordings, and new calls arrive on their own. You can also AirDrop a recording.

How do I delete everything? Delete the app: its recordings, transcripts and notes are stored only inside it. Reminders and calendar events you added from it stay in those apps, and files you exported stay where you saved them.
